I. BAI TOAN DAT RA

1. Bai toan gi

AI Hospital Dispatcher la bai toan dieu phoi robot trong benh vien tren ban do luoi 20x15.
Robot bat dau tai o START, can di den cac o benh nhan/task, tranh tuong, can nhac o trong so, tram sac, vat can dong, deadline va muc pin.

Gia tri o tren ban do:
- 0: San, cost di chuyen = 1.
- 1: Tuong, robot khong di qua duoc.
- 2: O trong so, cost di chuyen = 5.
- 3: Tram sac, robot co the sac lai pin.

Muc tieu cua chuong trinh la minh hoa 6 nhom thuat toan AI trong cung mot moi truong game Pygame:
- Tim duong co ban.
- Tim duong co heuristic.
- Toi uu thu tu task va pin.
- Lap ke hoach khi moi truong khong chac chan.
- Xep lich task bang rang buoc CSP.
- Ra quyet dinh doi khang voi moi truong dong.

2. PEAS cua bai toan

Performance:
- Hoan thanh task/benh nhan.
- Duong di ngan, cost thap.
- It node expanded, runtime_ms nho.
- Khong het pin, it sac lai.
- Tranh va cham voi vat can dong.
- Hoan thanh task co deadline/priority cao.

Environment:
- Ban do luoi 20 cot x 15 hang.
- San, tuong, o trong so, tram sac.
- Nhieu task/benh nhan.
- Vat can dong o cac level cao.
- Gioi han pin va thoi gian.

Actuators:
- Di chuyen len/xuong/trai/phai.
- Chon task tiep theo.
- Chon di toi tram sac.
- Re-plan khi duong bi chan.

Sensors:
- Vi tri robot.
- Ban do tinh.
- Vi tri task.
- Pin hien tai.
- Vat can dong quan sat duoc hoac du doan duoc.
- Deadline va priority cua task.

II. THUAT TOAN AP DUNG

1. Tong quan cac nhom thuat toan

Level 1 - Uninformed Search:
- BFS.
- DFS.
- UCS.

Level 2 - Informed Search:
- A* Search.
- IDA*.
- Greedy Best-First.

Level 3 - Local Search / Battery Optimization:
- Simple Hill Climbing.
- Local Beam Search.
- Simulated Annealing.

Level 4 - Planning under Uncertainty:
- AND-OR Search.
- Partial Observation.
- No Observation.

Level 5 - Constraint Satisfaction Problem:
- Backtracking Search.
- Forward Checking.
- Min-Conflicts.

Level 6 - Adversarial Search:
- Minimax.
- Alpha-Beta Pruning.
- Expectimax.

2. Trang thai bat dau va trang thai muc tieu

Trang thai bat dau:
- Robot o vi tri START cua level.
- Pin bang battery_limit cua map.
- Danh sach task chua hoan thanh.
- Ban do gom san, tuong, o trong so, tram sac va vat can dong neu co.

Trang thai muc tieu:
- Robot cham dung o task/benh nhan can phuc vu.
- Voi level co nhieu task, robot tao route qua cac task theo thu tu duoc thuat toan chon.
- Task duoc danh dau completed khi robot den dung o muc tieu.

3. Trinh bay mot thuat toan trong moi nhom

Nhom 1 - BFS:
- BFS dung queue FIFO.
- Mo rong node theo tung lop tu START.
- Khi gap goal thi reconstruct path.
- Dam bao duong ngan nhat theo so buoc neu moi buoc co cost bang nhau.

Nhom 2 - IDA*:
- IDA* ket hop DFS voi heuristic Manhattan.
- Dat nguong ban dau f_limit = h(start).
- Moi node co f(n) = g(n) + h(n).
- Neu f(n) > f_limit thi cat tia nhanh do.
- Sau moi vong, f_limit moi la gia tri f nho nhat da vuot nguong.
- Lap lai den khi tim thay duong toi task.

Nhom 3 - Local Beam Search:
- Duy tri dong thoi k lo trinh tot nhat, mac dinh k = 3.
- Moi lo trinh la mot thu tu task.
- Sinh cac lo trinh con bang cach doi cho task.
- Danh gia bang objective dua tren score, distance va so lan charge.
- Chi giu lai k lo trinh tot nhat toan cuc de lap tiep.

Nhom 4 - AND-OR Search:
- OR-node dai dien cho lua chon buoc di cua robot.
- AND-node dai dien cho cac kich ban vat can dong co the xay ra.
- Thuat toan tao conditional plan bang cach tranh cac vi tri vat can hien tai va du doan.
- Neu duong sap bi chan, game re-plan tu vi tri hien tai.

Nhom 5 - Forward Checking:
- Dung backtracking de xep thu tu task.
- Moi khi gan thu mot task vao prefix, thuat toan kiem tra truoc cac task con lai.
- Neu task tuong lai chac chan vi pham deadline hoac pin, nhanh do bi cat som.
- Giam so nhanh phai duyet so voi backtracking thuan.

Nhom 6 - Minimax:
- Moi action cua robot duoc danh gia trong cac trang thai moi truong: clear, crowded, blocked.
- Utility tinh reward, emergency bonus, distance penalty, collision penalty va battery penalty.
- Utility co xet battery hien tai.
- Khi pin yeu, action go_to_charge co diem tot hon nen duoc uu tien.

III. THUC NGHIEM VA KET QUA

1. Nhom thuat toan tim kiem mu thong tin

Level 1 dung BFS, DFS, UCS tren cung ban do Basic Delivery:
- BFS: tim duong ngan theo so buoc, mo rong theo lop.
- DFS: di sau theo nhanh uu tien, co the duong dai hon BFS.
- UCS: chon node co tong cost thap nhat, phu hop khi co o trong so.

2. Anh dong cua 3 thuat toan

Trong chuong trinh Pygame, khi chon tung thuat toan va bam RUN:
- Cac node visited duoc ve bang hieu ung song loang.
- Path cuoi cung duoc ve mau vang.
- Robot tu dong di theo path toi task.

Co the chup anh/gif demo cho cac thuat toan:
- BFS level 1.
- DFS level 1.
- UCS level 1.
- A* Search level 2.
- IDA* level 2.
- Greedy Best-First level 2.

3. Ket qua kiem thu nhanh sau cap nhat

Tat ca thuat toan trong 6 level deu tra ve cac truong bat buoc:
- path.
- nodes_expanded.
- runtime_ms.
- success.

Ket qua smoke test:
- Level 1: BFS, DFS, UCS deu success.
- Level 2: A* Search, IDA*, Greedy Best-First deu success.
- Level 3: Simple Hill Climbing, Local Beam Search, Simulated Annealing deu success.
- Level 4: AND-OR Search, Partial Observation, No Observation deu success.
- Level 5: Backtracking Search, Forward Checking, Min-Conflicts deu success.
- Level 6: Minimax, Alpha-Beta Pruning, Expectimax deu success va co xet pin hien tai.

IV. DANH GIA VA THAO LUAN

1. So sanh trong tung nhom

Bang so sanh tong quan:

Nhom | Thuat toan | Uu diem | Nhuoc diem
Uninformed | BFS | Duong ngan theo so buoc | Mo rong nhieu node
Uninformed | DFS | De cai dat, it bo nho hon BFS | De di vong, khong toi uu
Uninformed | UCS | Toi uu theo cost | Cham hon khi nhieu node
Informed | A* | Can bang cost va heuristic | Can heuristic tot
Informed | IDA* | It bo nho hon A*, co f-limit | Co the lap lai nhieu lan
Informed | Greedy | Nhanh, it node hon | Khong dam bao toi uu
Local | Hill Climbing | Don gian, nhanh | De ket local optimum
Local | Beam Search | Giu nhieu ung vien, on dinh hon | Ton chi phi danh gia hon Hill Climbing
Local | Annealing | Co the thoat local optimum | Phu thuoc nhiet do va random
Uncertainty | AND-OR | Co conditional plan | Mo hinh hoa moi truong phuc tap
Uncertainty | Partial Obs | Thuc te khi chi quan sat gan | Co the bo sot vat can xa
Uncertainty | No Obs | Minh hoa belief state | Kho toi uu, thong tin rat it
CSP | Backtracking | Dung ban chat CSP | De no nhanh
CSP | Forward Checking | Cat nhanh som | Can ham look-ahead tot
CSP | Min-Conflicts | Nhanh khi gan dung | Khong dam bao tim nghiem toi uu
Adversarial | Minimax | Bao thu voi truong hop xau | Ton node
Adversarial | Alpha-Beta | Giam node so voi Minimax | Phu thuoc thu tu duyet
Adversarial | Expectimax | Phu hop moi truong xac suat | Can xac suat hop ly

2. Y kien nhom

Viec ap dung 6 nhom thuat toan vao cung bai toan giup thay ro su khac nhau giua cac cach tiep can AI:
- Search co ban phu hop bai toan tim duong don gian.
- Informed search giam node expanded bang heuristic.
- Local search phu hop bai toan toi uu thu tu task va pin.
- Planning under uncertainty can thiet khi co vat can dong va quan sat khong day du.
- CSP phu hop khi task co deadline, priority va rang buoc nang luong.
- Adversarial search phu hop khi robot phai ra quyet dinh trong moi truong co rui ro.

Trong game, moi level khong chi la man choi ma con la mot minh hoa truc quan cho cach thuat toan suy nghi.
Hieu ung song loang giup nguoi xem thay node duoc mo rong, con path vang giup thay solution cuoi cung.
